# Test-set notes (cases 9-12)

Do not open before the Phase 0b end gate if you are building the layout. These notes are kept here, not in `AUTHOR-NOTES.md`, so that file can be read during the build.

1. **Case 9 (555 astable).** NE555P from a 9 V battery: RA 1k (VCC to DISCH), RB 10k (DISCH to THRES/TRIG), 10 uF timing capacitor, about 6.9 Hz (1.44 / ((RA + 2 RB) C)). RESET tied to VCC, 10 nF on CONT, 100 nF supply decoupling (no `decouples` hint, since SPEC 15 allows hints only on case 7). OUT drives a red LED through 470 ohm.
2. **Case 10 (bridge + 7805).** The transformer is drawn as an AC source (`value` 12 V RMS, `label` "12 V AC"), because transformers are Phase 2 symbols. The bridge is four 1N4007 diodes; its negative output is the circuit's `GND`, and AC1 and AC2 are signal nets, since the secondary floats. The unregulated rail `VUNREG` has `kind: "power"` set explicitly (not a default power name). LM7805 capacitors follow the datasheet's test conditions (0.22 uF in, 0.1 uF out); a green LED with 330 ohm shows the 5 V output.
3. **Case 11 (Uno substitute) has 24 parts and 96 pins, not 30 parts.** That is the substitute's main parts plus realistic supporting parts: 9 V input header, power switch, 1N4007 reverse-polarity diode and 100 uF on VIN; 100 nF at the LCD (no external I2C pull-ups: the Waveshare wiki FAQ says the module has its own on SDA and SCL); DHT22 10k pull-up and 100 nF (DHT22 pin 3 is marked no-connect); buzzer driven by a 2N2222A with 1k/10k base resistors and a 1N4148 flyback diode; push button to 5V with 10k pull-down and 100 nF; green and red status LEDs with 330 ohm; 10k threshold potentiometer on A0 with 100 nF. Padding it with parts the circuit does not need would make it less real. Gate 6's 30-part timing target can still be measured on it, or on a generated 30-part circuit.
4. **Case 11 connects the Uno's GND as the physical pin `A1[JA-6]`**, not the group `A1{GND}`, because the group's representative is `ICSP1-6` (AUTHOR-NOTES item 9). The 5V net uses `A1[JA-5]` (+5V on the power header); the LCD's SDA and SCL go to the R3 header pins `JD-17` and `JD-18` (grouped with A4 and A5).
5. **Case 11's add-part edit** (47 uF from 5V to GND) affects 20 of 25 parts by the SPEC 9.5 table, leaving 5 untouched (AUTHOR-NOTES item 23). Its remove-part edit affects 15 of 23.
6. **Case 12 (ESP32 substitute).** 12 V input through a 3 A fuse with 470 uF bulk; a Pololu D24V10F5 makes 5 V for the DevKitC's 5V pin; IO25 drives an AO3400A low-side switch (its Rds(on) is specified at Vgs 2.5 V, so a 3.3 V GPIO turns it fully on; a TO-220 IRLZ44N is only specified from 4 V) (100 ohm gate, 10k pull-down) on the LED strip's return; the status LED is on IO4 with 330 ohm. The module's PG and SHDN pins are unconnected: the Pololu page says SHDN has an internal pull-up and may float, and PG is an optional open-drain output. Their roles (`output`, `input`) do not trigger the unconnected-supply warning. As the Espressif guide warns, the board must then not also be powered from USB; that is an operating rule, not a part of the drawing.
